Privacy

Keeps cookies and cache on disk, so accounts survive a restart and pages load faster. Veil has no browsing history either way. Needs a restart.
Breaks signing in to Google. YouTube keeps its advert list inside the watch page, where the player reads it before anything else can run; reaching it means Veil answering https requests itself rather than letting Chromium make them. Google's account endpoints refuse any request re-issued that way — 401, malformed — and no amount of header handling changes it. It removes adverts from a watch page opened directly, and not from one you click through to inside YouTube. Off unless you want that trade.
Adds per-site noise to canvas, WebGL and audio readings, so sites cannot join them into one identity. This scrambles you rather than making you look like everyone else — only Tor Browser and Mullvad Browser do the latter.
Says 8 cores and 8 GB regardless of the real machine. Turn off if a site misbehaves.
Notifications and device APIs are always denied.
