Veil

Keeps nothing, shows nobody.

What it does not do

There is no history feature at all — nothing records the pages you visit.
Ad and tracker domains are dropped before they leave the machine.
No crash reporting, metrics or background pings.
Nothing summarises, suggests or calls a model.

Search

DuckDuckGo and Marginalia are queried over a cookie-less session, merged by reciprocal rank fusion, stripped of redirect wrappers and tracking parameters. Wikipedia supplies the answer card.
