Import

Bring your bookmarks and your saved logins over from another browser. Nothing leaves this machine: the files are read here and written straight into Veil.

Bookmarks

Looking…
An exported bookmarks .html from any browser — Firefox and Safari included — or a Chromium Bookmarks file.

Passwords

Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari and the password managers all export one. Veil reads it and writes the logins into your vault, encrypted.
  1. In your other browser, open its password settings.
  2. Export passwords to a CSV file. It will ask you to confirm who you are.
  3. Choose that file here.
  4. Delete the CSV afterwards. It is every password you have, in plain text.

What Veil will not import

There is nowhere to put it. Veil has no history feature at all — no database, no interface, nothing recording the pages you visit. Importing somebody else's record of that would be the one thing this browser exists not to do.
Signing in again is a few minutes; a copied session cookie is a copy of your account sitting in two places.
Veil does not run extensions from a shop. It carries uBlock Origin itself, which is the one most people were installing.
